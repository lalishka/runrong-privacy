# RUNRONG Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 November 2026

**Who we are:** RUNRONG is made by Lali Orudjova, an individual developer (“we”, “us”). She is the data controller.

**Contact:** [lalishkaa@gmail.com](mailto:lalishkaa@gmail.com), or the support form inside the app.

**App:** RUNRONG for Android and iOS (`dev.runrong`).

## 1. In short

- No accounts. We do not run servers that receive your data.
- Your plan, workouts, routes and settings are stored **on your phone**.
- Location is used **only during a workout you start**.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We do not sell your data.
- A few outside services are used for maps, the subscription and support. They are listed in section 5.

## 2. Age

RUNRONG is for adults **18 and over**. The app asks you to confirm this at the start. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly process children’s data. If you think a child has sent us information, write to us and we will delete it.

## 3. What is stored on your phone

- Your 18+ confirmation and your plan choices (goal, program, schedule, experience).
- Optional answers you give, such as how hard a session felt or whether something hurt. These are notes in the app, not a medical record.
- Workout history: date, duration, distance, pace, and the GPS route if one was recorded.
- Map progress made from your routes, and a home zone if you set one. The home zone is removed from files you export or share.
- Settings: units, voice, reminders, vibration, Health Connect.

We do not ask for your name, email or phone number. Voice cues are recorded audio bundled with the app or your phone’s own speech engine. The app does not use the microphone.

## 4. Permissions

You can say no to any permission. The workout timer still works.

**Location.** If you allow it, precise location is used to measure distance and pace and to draw your route — **only while a workout you started is running**. The app may keep using location for that workout when the screen is off or you switch apps (on iPhone you will see the location indicator; on Android a workout notification is shown). When the workout ends, location stops. The app never tracks you outside a workout.

**Notifications, lock screen and Live Activity.** If you allow notifications, the app can send reminders on the days and times you choose. During a workout, a lock-screen card (iPhone Live Activity) or a workout notification (Android) shows the timer and progress. All of this is created on the phone and is not sent to us.

**Health Connect (Android, optional).** Only after you agree:
- *Read:* your exercise sessions from the last 28 days (walking, running or other; start and end time; how they were recorded) and, if you also allow it, their distance. The app does not read routes, notes, heart rate or medical records.
- *Write:* if you turn it on in Settings, finished workouts are saved as exercise sessions (type, start, end, distance). Routes are not written.

You can turn this off in RUNRONG or in Health Connect at any time. Data already in Health Connect stays there until you delete it there.

**Apple Health (iPhone, optional).** The app only asks to *save* workouts. It does not ask to read any Health data.

RUNRONG has no direct Samsung Health integration.

**Photos.** Only to save a card you chose. The app does not read your photo library.

## 5. Outside services

- **OpenStreetMap maps.** To show a map, the app downloads map images from `tile.openstreetmap.org`. Like any web request, this shows that server your IP address and the map area being viewed. Your route itself is not uploaded. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors; see the [OSMF privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
- **Apple App Store / Google Play.** They handle downloads, payments and subscriptions under their own privacy policies.
- **RevenueCat.** We use RevenueCat to sell RUNRONG Pro and check whether it is active (including Restore purchases). RevenueCat receives a random app user ID (not your name or email), your purchase history from the store and your subscription status. See [RevenueCat’s privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). RevenueCat is in the United States, so this data may be processed there.
- **Support form (Google Forms).** If you contact us through the in-app form, Google receives what you type, plus the app version and platform the app fills in for you. We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed for that. See [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The same applies if you email us.
- **Links you open.** Reference articles, Apple Music or Spotify open in your browser or that app. We do not send your workout data with them.

We do **not** use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, session-recording or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

## 6. Export, delete and backups

- **Export:** you can save a backup file or a CSV of your runs, or share a card, using the phone’s share sheet. Whoever receives the file sees its contents. The backup file is not password-protected.
- **Delete:** you can delete single runs or use **Settings → Delete all data**. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. Deleting data does not cancel a subscription; purchases stay with the store and can be restored.
- **Backups:** RUNRONG data is excluded from cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer. Use export if you want to keep a copy.

## 7. How to delete your data

This section is for the RUNRONG app by Lali Orudjova.

**Data on your phone (workouts, plan, settings, map progress):**
1. Open RUNRONG → **Settings → Delete all data**.
2. Confirm. Everything RUNRONG stored on the phone is deleted at once and cannot be restored.
3. Or simply uninstall the app: this also deletes its data.

**Data held outside the phone:**
- **Support messages** (what you sent through the in-app form or by email): write to [lalishkaa@gmail.com](mailto:lalishkaa@gmail.com) with the subject “Delete my data”. We delete them within 30 days and confirm by email if you gave us one.
- **RevenueCat record** (random app user ID, purchase history, subscription status): ask in the same email. We delete the record within 30 days. Records the store needs for your purchase stay with Apple or Google (see below).

**What is kept:**
- Purchases and subscription records stay with Apple App Store or Google Play under their rules; deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the store settings.
- Workouts you saved to Health Connect or Apple Health stay there until you delete them there.
- Files you exported or shared stay wherever you saved or sent them.

## 8. Legal basis (EU/UK)

- Location and app features you use: to provide the app you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), with your permission given in the phone settings.
- Health Connect / Apple Health: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in the system settings.
- Subscription via the store and RevenueCat: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
- Support requests: our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f)).

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR, UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Uzbekistan’s personal-data law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object or withdraw consent. Most of your data is only on your phone, so you can see, export and delete it yourself in the app. For data held by us (support messages) or to ask about RevenueCat records linked to your app user ID, write to [lalishkaa@gmail.com](mailto:lalishkaa@gmail.com). You can also complain to your local data-protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

## 10. Security

Your data is protected by your phone’s app sandbox. Use a screen lock. Data sent to outside services goes over HTTPS.

## 11. Not medical advice

RUNRONG is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat anything.

## 12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date. Important changes will also be noted in the app or the store listing.
