# RUNRONG Privacy Policy
Effective date: 2026-10-23
Developer: Lali Orudjova ("we"). Contact: lalishka@gmail.com. App ID: dev.runrong.

## 1. Summary
RUNRONG has no accounts, no servers of our own, no ads and no analytics. Your workout data is stored only on your device. We do not receive, sell or share it.

## 2. Data processed on your device
- **Location (precise)** — only while a workout is running (foreground), to calculate distance, pace and draw your route. No background location. Stored locally in workout history.
- **Workout data** — programs, timer progress, distance, pace, duration, route, history, settings.
- **Notifications** — workout cues/reminders; no data leaves the device.
- **Health integrations (write-only):** with your permission RUNRONG writes completed workouts (type, time, duration, distance, route where supported) to Android Health Connect (and through it Samsung Health) or Apple Health (HealthKit). RUNRONG does not read health data. You can revoke access in Health Connect / the Health app at any time.

## 3. Third parties
- **OpenStreetMap map tiles.** To show your route map, the app downloads map images from OpenStreetMap tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org; © OpenStreetMap contributors). Like any web request, this exposes your IP address, the requested map area and app/device info to that provider, governed by its policy (https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy). Your route itself is not sent.
- **App stores** (Apple, Google) process downloads and crash data under their own policies.
- No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never transferred to third parties.

## 4. Retention and deletion
Data stays until you delete workouts in the app or uninstall the app. Data written to Health Connect / Apple Health is managed there. Since we hold no data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server; contact us with any questions.

## 5. Children
RUNRONG is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA/UK where required) and is intended for ages 13+. We do not knowingly collect children's data. If you believe a child provided data to us, contact lalishka@gmail.com and we will help delete it.

## 6. Your rights
- **GDPR / UK GDPR:** processing occurs on your device under your control; legal basis for OS-level permissions is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 9(2)(a) for health data), which you may withdraw in system settings. You may contact us or your supervisory authority.
- **CCPA/CPRA (California):** we do not sell or share personal information and do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics.
- **Russia (152-FZ):** we do not collect or store personal data of Russian citizens on our servers; data stays on the user's device, so no cross-border transfer or database localization by us occurs.

## 7. Security
Data is protected by your device's OS sandbox. Use a device lock.

## 8. Not medical advice
RUNRONG is not a medical device. Consult a doctor before training.

## 9. Changes
We will update this page and the effective date; material changes will be noted in the app/store listing.
